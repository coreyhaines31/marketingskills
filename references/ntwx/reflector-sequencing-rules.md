# Reflector Sales Sequencing — NTWX Canonical

**Source:** reflector.ml-nightworx.io Sales Cockpit  
**Strategy:** Porter's Differentiation Focus — narrow AU white-label AI commerce segment  
**Owner archetypes:** GENE (FDE) primary; ATLAS (Phase 1); CEDAR (Phase 2); SIENNA (Phase 3); PROMETHEUS (Phases 5–6)

## Three non-negotiable rules

1. **Never send the audit cold** — Send the questionnaire first. Let them acknowledge the gap. Then the audit is "here's what I found about that gap" — not "here's what's wrong with you."

2. **Never show tech before gap is acknowledged** — Showing tech to a prospect who hasn't acknowledged a problem = "nice to have" = no urgency. Phase 3 (audit) must come before Phase 5 (solution).

3. **Never lead with price** — If they anchor on $499 before understanding value = "too expensive." Lead with finding, then moat, then ROI. Price comes after value.

## Six-phase funnel

| Phase | Name | Owner | Prospect interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Target | ATLAS + GENE | None — internal intel |
| 2 | Discovery | CEDAR + GENE | 6-question email or 15-min call |
| 3 | Audit | SIENNA + GENE | Pre-filled draft for validation |
| 4 | Decision-maker | GENE | Forwardable one-pager + 15-min meeting |
| 5 | Solution | GENE + PROMETHEUS | 10-min live demo |
| 6 | Close & Launch | GENE + PROMETHEUS | 48h operational handoff |

## Qualify IN

- AU retailers with 100+ SKUs
- Digital agencies serving AU retailers
- Comparison publishers in AU commerce
- Businesses concerned about data sovereignty

## Disqualify EARLY

- Price-sensitive (not cheapest)
- No product catalog (no RAG to build on)
- Want to build AI in-house
- Non-AU (sovereignty moat doesn't apply)

## Magic-wand rule

Discovery question #5: *"If you could wave a magic wand and AI solved one problem in 90 days — what would it be?"*

Whatever they answer is the pitch axis for Phases 4–5.

## Decision-maker meeting (15 minutes)

| Minutes | Frame |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | "I looked at your setup. Here's what I found." — 3 findings: technical, compliance, analytics |
| 2–5 | "Here's what closing that gap looks like." — Magic-wand answer + ROI; savings or growth first? |
| 5–9 | Three moats only: (1) AI agent + RAG, (2) Sovereign data room, (3) 48-hour launch |
| 9–12 | Data question: AU centres, no OpenAI, Vertex AI AU, Data Ownership Addendum |
| 12–15 | Operational handoff: one form → 10 min provision → 48h live → book provisioning session |

## Close frame

> This isn't a consulting engagement. There's no hourly billing, no open-ended scope. You fill in one form, we provision in 10 minutes, RAG trains automatically, ASTROX validates compliance, and you're live in 48 hours. You own your data. You can leave anytime. The only thing we ask is that if we help you make money, you share 5% of it with us.

## Eight moat layers (use 3 in decision-maker meeting; all 8 in internal prep)

1. Conversational AI agent (3-stage hybrid scoring)
2. Per-tenant RAG isolation (>90% accuracy gate)
3. White-label invisibility (ML-NTWX never appears)
4. <48 hour launch (7-step automation)
5. AU Privacy Act APP 1–13 (ASTROX gate)
6. Fixed-price + revenue share ($499/mo + 5% GMV)
7. GEO/AEO schema generation
8. Revenue-aligned incentives (platform wins when partner wins)
